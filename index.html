Math Visualizations

Interactive pictures for selected topics. Change the parameters and watch the mathematics respond; every page keeps its state in the URL, so a link reproduces what you see.

  • Riemann sums

    How the integral ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx arises as the limit of rectangle sums over finer and finer partitions, squeezed between lower and upper sums.

  • Fundamental theorem of calculus

    Slide b and watch the area ∫ₐᵇ f build the antiderivative F, whose tangent at b has slope exactly f(b).

  • Riemann zeta function

    ζ(x) = Σ 1/kˣ for real x > 1, and how the partial sums 1 + 1/2ˣ + … + 1/nˣ creep up to it as n grows.

  • Exponential functions

    f(x) = a·ebx+c + d: what each parameter does, tangents that always meet the asymptote 1/b away, growth and decay.

  • Surfaces z = f(x, y)

    A 3D plotter with slices, tangent planes, geodesics and their acceleration vectors.
